About

Jonah Cullen is a songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer who blends progressive rock and metal with pop sensibilities and a range of dynamics that capture a bredth of life experiences: the mistakes, the beauty, the loss, the hope, and the doubt.

The 2024 release, "Within," represents one dynamic half of the range of his sound, with a mix of emotive orchestral ballads, upbeat brassy pop songs, and dreamy soundscapes. The upcoming sister album, "Without," will form the other bookend of this dynamic spectrum, painting with a progressive metal palette while still emphasizing melody and emotion to create a more intense, cinimatic, and technical listening experience.

Prior to this series of solo records, Cullen was a member of the Boise progressive/alternative rock power trio, LUX, along with Sam Harmon and Alisha Peru. Their album, "Lux Aeterna," captures a younger and more raw sound that harkens back to the nostalgic sounds of the 90s.

Press & bio

Jonah Cullen is a songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer with a focus on cinematic, emotive, and technical music both soft and heavy. His live shows bring the elaborate and lush productions of the studio albums to life through dynamic performances with the help of talented musicians in the DMV area. The dymaic range of Cullen's music makes him an easy addition to any bill, but he is most at-home in a progressive rock/metal setting. His first solo record, "Within," was released in 2024 and can be listened to now anywhere you listen to music with several more albums on the way.
